# OpenCap post-beta priorities

UDEXREAL lists UDCAP Driver 0.1.8.7 as its download. This comparison uses the vendor's documented workflows, the OpenCap Windows workspace, and the existing hardware acceptance notes. It does not include a new side-by-side hardware run. [UDCAP downloads](https://udexreal.com/pages/setup) · [OpenCap compatibility and hardware notes](parity.md)

## What this build changes

The SteamVR skeleton now targets a 50° index-to-pinky spread at full calibrated extension with the default hand response curves. The three adjacent gaps contribute 18°, 14°, and 18°, up from 12°, 10°, and 12°. The desktop hand preview renders those same skeleton transforms and shows each gap as a percentage of its captured range. A gap with fewer than 26 counts between the two poses is marked Weak. UDCAP's separate calibration grades use higher thresholds, so a gap can produce a reading and still receive a Poor grade.

Existing calibration captures and hand curves are read automatically. Custom curve values still shape their respective gaps, and the wider mapping scales their SteamVR output as well. VMC and legacy OSC use their own angle conversions. A SteamVR avatar or game may retarget or limit the pose after it leaves OpenCap. [Valve skeletal input documentation](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr/blob/master/docs/Driver_API_Documentation.md#skeletal-input)

## Release priorities

First, compare the together, half-spread, full-spread, and relaxed fist poses with both hands in the preview and on the same SteamVR avatar. Record the glove and controller revisions, firmware, three spread readings, and the active tracker. When the preview opens fully and the avatar does not, check binding and avatar retargeting. When a meter tops out early or shows Weak, inspect the calibration capture first.

Beta 2 includes single-glove guided calibration, live learning and timestamped sensor recordings. Next, compare glove and tracker timing during dropouts and collect labeled spread gestures from more wearers. Use those recordings to check how quickly calibration recovers after a fit change.

OpenCap already has settings migration and reversible SteamVR setup. Combined-receiver and controller firmware coverage, a clean Windows install, and in-headset acceptance remain compatibility work. Verify them separately from new features. [Compatibility matrix](parity.md)
